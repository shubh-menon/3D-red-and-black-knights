KNIGHTS RESEARCH — 1.1.0
Windows 64-bit | Unity 6000.6.3f1 | 13 October 2026

Open "Knights Research.exe". Keep the executable and all accompanying files together.

The View & camera panel opens by default at the left side of the screen.
Panels remain draggable. Use the toolbar to toggle individual panels, or press
U to hide/restore the complete interface. Starting a run hides the interface and
waits one second before placing dots; press U to show the controls during a run.

This release uses neutral application, assembly, code namespace and export names.
The king has 26 neighbours. Planar bishops attack along 12 face-diagonal rays;
planar queens attack along those rays plus the 6 axis directions. Knights have
24 signed-permutation moves. The greedy placement rules are unchanged.

CSV exports and their metadata are saved under:
%USERPROFILE%\AppData\LocalLow\Independent Research\Knights Research\ResearchLogs\Runs

Custom-piece presets are stored under the same root in ResearchPresets.

Validation: 11 simulation configurations covering 1,361,000 placements retained
identical complete research tables after the export-identifier rename. Rule,
CSV, interface, exact rendering, preview rendering and metadata checks passed.
